SHOTS

  • Swing = high deep clear.
  • Hold toward net + Swing = soft drop shot.
  • Hold away + Swing = extra-deep clear.
  • Smash while the shuttle is high (jump!) = steep fast kill. Low smash becomes a flat drive.
  • Timing: hit when the shuttle is at your racket for PERFECT (more accurate, charges special).
  • Serve: Swing = long high serve, hold toward net = short serve (must pass the short service line).
Rally-point scoring · win by 2 · points go to whoever wins the rally, and they serve next. Out, into the net, or landing in your own court loses the rally. Gamepads supported (X swing, Y smash, A jump, B dive, RB special). Esc pauses, M mutes.
